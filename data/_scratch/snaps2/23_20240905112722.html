Bulletin

As you prepare to head home, keep these things in mind: -Plan to take a bit of extra time to MOOP sweep your camp. Be sure to properly secure your loads to your vehicles. -If you can’t see, don’t drive. Driving in low visibility is dangerous for you and others. -Take it slow, be patient, and wait at your camp until conditions improve before heading to Gate Road. During dust storms, Gate Road often has worse visibility than inside Black Rock City, and Exodus travel times might be delayed. -Watch the BRC Dashboard and listen to BMIR 94.5 FM for updated weather information throughout the day. -Feeling curious?
