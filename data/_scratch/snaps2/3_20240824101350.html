Bulletin

Weather Update - Friday 8/23/24 7:22pm --- Weather forecasts indicate rain may start around 3am tonight and run through Saturday morning. We do not expect large amounts of rain, though rainfall would impact ingress to Black Rock City. We believe that if the rain is imminent around 3am, we may close Gate Road. Whenever it starts raining, we will close the entrance to the event. Please plan accordingly! If Gate Road is closed due to rain, delay your travel to Black Rock City until we announce that the Gate has reopened. It is not safe to wait on the side of the highway on the way to BRC. We are pushing this out now to support good decision making, public safety, and so we all have the least possible impact on local communities. When you do travel to Black Rock City, please be safe and obey all traffic laws. If the Gate has to be closed, we will reopen it as soon as the roads are dry enough to do so. We will communicate through the BRC Dashboard, on BMIR 94.5 FM (also available on iHeartRadio), and on our Facebook page as soon as we can reopen. The forecast and our on-site meteorologist indicate Monday and through the rest of the week are going to be warm and dry. We’re excited for all the joy and awe that’s going to come to life in Black Rock City this year. See you in the dust!
